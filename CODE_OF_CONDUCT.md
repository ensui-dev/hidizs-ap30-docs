# Code of conduct

This project documents the Hidizs AP30: its hardware, firmware and recovery. People come here with very different
backgrounds, from first-time owners with a player that won't start to people who read firmware for a living. This
page says how we treat each other in the repository, its issues, discussions and pull requests, and on the site.

## What we expect

* **Be kind and patient.** Many readers are new to embedded hardware. A question that looks basic is a good question;
  answer it, or link the page that does.
* **Assume good faith.** Disagree with a claim, not with the person. Point to the source, measurement or firmware
  version that supports your view.
* **Stay on topic.** The AP30, its firmware and closely related devices and tools.
* **Be careful with other people's devices.** Never suggest a step that writes to a player without saying what can
  go wrong and how to recover. Never push someone to try something risky "to see what happens".
* **Credit others.** Say where a finding comes from, including other community projects.

## What is not accepted

* Insults, harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, sexual content, or personal attacks of any kind.
* **Personal data:** posting anyone's name, e-mail address, location, photos of people, device serial numbers or
  the contents of someone's backup without their clear consent, or trying to find out who someone is.
* Deliberately misleading instructions, or advice meant to damage someone's device.
* Links to pirated software, leaked vendor source code or other material shared without permission. (Content rules
  for the docs themselves are in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md#rules-for-content-same-as-the-readme).)
* Spam, advertising, and repeated off-topic posts after being asked to stop.

## Reporting

If someone breaks these rules, or you feel unsafe, report it **privately**:

* **To the maintainers:** open a private report through the repository's **Security** tab → **Report a
  vulnerability** (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)), start the title with "Conduct:", and link the post or posts
  concerned. Only the maintainers can see it.
* **To GitHub:** for something that breaks GitHub's own rules (harassment, spam, illegal content), also use the
  **Report content** entry in the post's **…** menu, which goes to GitHub.

Reports are read by the maintainers only and handled confidentially. Nobody is punished for a report made in good
faith.

## What maintainers may do

Depending on what happened: a friendly reminder, editing or hiding a post (with a note saying why), locking a
thread, a temporary block, or a permanent ban from the repository. Maintainers apply these rules to themselves too.

## Scope

These rules apply in this repository (issues, discussions, pull requests, commits, the wiki if one is added) and
anywhere someone represents the project. They do not apply to Hidizs, whose products and support channels are
outside this project; this project is not affiliated with Hidizs.
